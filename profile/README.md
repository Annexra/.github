# Annexra

We build websites, apps, and LMS and ERP systems for businesses.

## What we do

- **Websites:** fast, clean sites that look good and are easy to manage.
- **Apps:** web and mobile apps built around how your business works.
- **LMS:** learning platforms for courses, students, progress and certificates.
- **ERP:** tools that bring sales, operations, people and reports into one place.
